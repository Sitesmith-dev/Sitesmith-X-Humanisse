# Humanisse

## Register
brand (marketing landing page; the design is the product)

## Platform
web (Next.js App Router, CSS Modules, Motion library, lucide-react icons)

## Purpose
Humanisse teaches life and professional skills (negotiation, resilience, ethics, emotional intelligence) through original comics starring the Professor, the Bot and the Cat. The landing page exists to get a visitor to buy a comic or the complete set.

## Users
Mostly on phones. Students, early-career people, team leads and teachers; curious, short on time, arriving from a link. Mobile first, always.

## Tone
Warm, calm, a little playful. Direction B's candle-lit reading room for mood, Direction A's flat colour, ink borders and pop shadows for liveliness.

## Strategic rules (from client feedback, October 2026)
- No more than about two screens of scroll on the landing page
- Few sections, no repeated imagery
- Every section leads toward buying a comic
- Motion is purposeful and quiet; honour prefers-reduced-motion

## Anti-references
Long scrolling marketing pages, repeated feature grids, generic SaaS cards, decorative looping motion.
